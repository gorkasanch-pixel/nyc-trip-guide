Getting Around

3 routes
NYC subway fares (2026): flat $3.00/ride via OMNY — just tap a contactless card or phone at the turnstile, no advance purchase needed. Kids 5–17 ride for $1.00 when tapping in with a paying adult. Fares cap automatically at $35/person per rolling 7-day period on the same card.

LaGuardia → Marriott Marquis

~25–40 MIN · TAXI / RIDESHARE RECOMMENDED

With four people and luggage landing at 2 PM, a taxi or rideshare (roughly $40–65) is the simplest option. A subway/bus combination exists but involves a transfer — not worth the hassle with bags on arrival day.

Marriott Marquis → Yankee Stadium

~20–25 MIN · D TRAIN · LEAVE BY 6:00 PM
  1. Walk ~5 minutes to 47-50 Sts–Rockefeller Center (B/D/F/M, on 6th Ave at 50th St).
  2. Take the D train uptown, toward Norwood–205 St.
  3. Ride to 161 St–Yankee Stadium — the stadium is right across the street.
Alternative: walk to Times Sq–42 St, take the free S shuttle to Grand Central, then the 4 train uptown to the same 161 St–Yankee Stadium stop.

Marriott Marquis → USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center

~25–30 MIN · 7 TRAIN, NO TRANSFER · LEAVE BY 10:00–10:15 AM
  1. Walk to Times Sq–42 St station.
  2. Take the 7 train toward Flushing–Main St.
  3. Ride to Mets–Willets Point — you'll walk directly into the grounds.
Bag policy: max ~12"×12"×16", no glass bottles. Empty refillable bottles are fine — free water refill stations are on the grounds.

  • 11:00 AM

    Day session begins

    US Open opening day of the main draw. With kids, the outer courts (Grandstand, Court 17, practice courts) are often more engaging than a long sit in Arthur Ashe.

  • Midday

    Optional break in the park

    The Unisphere and New York Hall of Science are both a short walk from the grounds — good for a change of pace between matches.

  • Evening

    Dinner in Flushing

    One stop past the tennis center on the 7 train — one of the best food neighborhoods in the city.

  • 9:30 AM

    American Museum of Natural History

    Opens at 10 AM — dinosaur halls, the blue whale, and the Hayden Planetarium make for a solid 2.5–3 hour visit.

  • 1:00 PM

    Fred's or Playa Betty's are both a short walk from the museum.

  • 2:30 PM

    Central Park

    Right next door — the carousel, Central Park Zoo, or a rowboat at Loeb Boathouse are easy, low-key options for the second half of the day.

  • Evening

    Dinner near the hotel + early night

    Tomorrow's an early departure — worth packing before bed.

Tue Sep 1 — departure: with an arrival back in Omaha around 10:00 AM, plan to leave the hotel for LaGuardia by roughly 7:00–7:30 AM to allow for a domestic flight (check-in, security, and taxi/rideshare time).
